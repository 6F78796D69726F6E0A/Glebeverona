Welcome

Set within a heritage Victorian residence on Glebe Point Road, Verona House is close to local cafés and shops, Sydney University, RPA Hospital, waterfront parks, public transport and Sydney's CBD. Glebe is known for its heritage streetscapes, independent cafés, markets and village atmosphere.

Verona House is a multi-room guesthouse. Your booking is for your private room and ensuite; entryways and designated common areas are shared with other guests.

House Rules

  • No parties, events, gatherings or unapproved visitors.
  • Maximum occupancy is two registered guests per room.
  • No pets.
  • No smoking or vaping inside the room, ensuite, building, hallways, entryways, balconies or verandah, or within 4 metres of an entrance or exit.
  • Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Please be considerate of other guests and neighbours.
  • No commercial photography, video production, media shoots or recordings without prior written approval.
  • No candles, incense, naked flames or illegal substances.
  • Do not enter staff, storage, reception cabinet, back-of-house, cleaner or locked areas.
  • Keep hallways, stairs, entryways and common areas clear of rubbish, food, shoes, bags and personal belongings.
  • Do not flush wipes, sanitary products, nappies, paper towels or foreign objects down the toilet.
  • Please turn off lights, television, heating/air-conditioning and appliances when leaving your room, and close windows.
Smoke odour, ash, cigarette butts or vaping residue may require specialised cleaning or odour treatment. Additional cleaning or damage costs may apply. Serious or repeated breaches may result in termination of the reservation where permitted by the booking platform.

Your Room & Access

Your room is private and includes its own ensuite bathroom. Verona House uses digital keypad access—there are no physical keys or lockboxes.

1. Enter the building

Walk through the front entrance until you reach the main door with the VERONA sign.

Wake the keypad by giving it a firm touch or pressing it with your palm until the screen lights up. Enter the 4-digit access code provided with your check-in instructions, then press #.

2. Enter your room

Each room has a digital keypad lock. Enter the same 4-digit access code provided with your check-in instructions, press the ✓ button, then turn the door knob.

Important reminders

  • The main building door locks automatically when it closes.
  • Your room door also locks automatically when it closes, so please remember your access code whenever you leave the room.
  • Please do not block hallways, stairs, entryways, fire exits or doors.

Refreshments, Kitchen & Laundry

There is no shared guest kitchen or laundry onsite. Your room includes light refreshment amenities such as a mini fridge, microwave, kettle, basic crockery and tea/coffee facilities.

Closest laundromat: Glebe Ezy Wash, 144C Glebe Point Road, Glebe NSW 2037.

Heating & Cooling

Heating and air-conditioning are operated by remote control. Please keep windows closed while the system is running and turn it off when leaving the room.

If the remote does not respond, spare AAA batteries are provided in the room. Replace both batteries together, observing the + and − markings. If the system still does not operate, please contact us through the booking platform.

TV & Entertainment

A Smart TV and remote control are provided. If the remote is not responding, spare batteries are available in your room. Please leave the remote in a visible place when you check out.

Garbage & Recycling

Guest-use bins are located at the side of the building. Exit through the front door, walk down the steps and turn immediately left.

  • Red bins: general rubbish.
  • Yellow bins: recycling.

Please place rubbish securely inside the bins and do not leave it in rooms, hallways, entryways, beside the bins or in common areas. If the bins are full, please contact us.

Parking

There is no dedicated onsite parking. Street parking is available nearby and is subject to local signage. The south side of Glebe Point Road has 2-hour unticketed parking Monday to Friday, and surrounding streets include 2-hour and some unrestricted spaces. Always check street signs before leaving your vehicle, as restrictions may change.

Check-Out

Before leaving, please:

  • Turn off lights, TV, heating/air-conditioning and appliances.
  • Close windows and make sure your room door is locked.
  • Leave used cups, dishes and crockery tidy.
  • Place rubbish in the appropriate bins at the side of the building.

If you would like to extend your stay, please contact us through the booking platform.
