04

Follow the water

From Verona House, make your way to Bellevue, then follow the bay to Tramsheds.

Bellevue to Tramsheds · Allow 60–90 minutes with stops

  1. Begin at Bellevue

    Find the historic house at 55–57 Leichhardt Street, beside Blackwattle Bay Park. It is home to Bellevue Cottage by Antoine; book ahead if you would like brunch before your walk. Pause to look across the water.

  2. Follow the foreshore

    Take the public waterside path around Glebe Point towards the end of Glebe Point Road. Look for views of the Anzac Bridge and the bays.

  3. Continue into the parklands

    Follow the shoreline towards Bicentennial Park, then turn inland towards Jubilee Park. Choose a bench or a patch of lawn for a break.

  4. Finish at Tramsheds

    Follow signed pedestrian routes towards Jubilee Park light rail and Dalgal Way. Tramsheds is a useful place to finish with something to eat.

Find the starting pointForeshore history

Timing is a relaxed planning estimate for the waterfront stretch; add time to reach Bellevue and return to Verona House. Follow local signs and any diversions. Paths are shared, and street approaches can be steep. Check access details if you need a step-free route.

06

Make a day of it

A few ideas, with room to wander.

A few hours

A gentle first taste

Start with coffee at Sonoma, stroll along Glebe Point Road and head down to the foreshore. Turn back whenever you feel ready; finish with a meal at The Toxteth.

A Saturday

Books, markets and the bay

Have breakfast at Dirty Red, explore Glebe Markets from 10 am and browse Gleebooks. In the afternoon, try the waterfront walk. Reserve Glebe Point Diner or No 92 for dinner, or finish with a glass of wine in Sappho’s courtyard.

A rainy day

Art and a long lunch

Browse Gleebooks, then visit the Chau Chak Wing Museum. If you prefer contemporary art, choose White Rabbit in Chippendale on one of its opening days. Leave time for a leisurely lunch.

Check opening days and bookings before you set out. Walking directions start at Verona House; you can change the starting point in Google Maps.

08

Good to know

A few practical details for an easier stay.

Find your bearings

Verona House is at 224 Glebe Point Road. Head towards Broadway for the markets and Gleebooks, or towards Glebe Point for the bays and waterfront parks.

Walk comfortably

Bring water and sun protection for a longer foreshore walk. Streets between the main road and the water can be hilly.

Take the light rail

The L1 Dulwich Hill Line links Central with Glebe and Jubilee Park. Use the Transport for NSW trip planner for departures and service changes.

Tap on and off

Use an Opal card or an accepted contactless card or device. Tap on and off with the same one: a phone and its physical bank card count separately. Payment information.

Pick up essentials

Broadway Sydney, 1 Bay Street, has shopping, dining, services and a cinema. Check the centre directory to find the store you need.

Plan your return

For a taxi or rideshare home, use Verona House, 224 Glebe Point Road, Glebe. Check evening transport before a late meal. Directions back to Verona House.

In an emergency, call 000 for police, fire or ambulance. Emergency information. For help with your stay, use the contact details supplied with your booking.

A neighbourhood with a story

Look a little closer

Glebe takes its name from land granted to the Anglican Church in 1789. Its waterfront later became a place of timber yards and industry. Community campaigning helped turn that shoreline into the public parks and paths enjoyed today.
